Welcome to Fusion!

What is Fusion?

Fusion is a TCG (Trading Card Game) where players collect and trade cards. TCG runs completely in your terminal!

Why choose TCG?

TCG is a unique and engaging way to experience the world of trading card games. It offers a nostalgic feel while providing a platform for players to connect and compete with others. With its simple yet strategic gameplay, TCG is perfect for both casual players and competitive gamers alike.

How to Play

To play, connect over telnet or with another TCP client. The server address is localhost:22
